Tab Suspender

Sorry to see you go

If you had suspended tabs open when you removed the extension, your browser closed them. Here is how to get them back.

Why this happens

Suspended tabs live on a page that belongs to the extension. When the extension is removed, that page no longer exists, so the browser closes those tabs. Your original URLs are not lost though, they are still in your history.

Quickest fix: reopen closed tabs

Press Ctrl+Shift+T (Cmd+Shift+T on Mac) repeatedly. This reopens recently closed tabs, often the fastest way to get a whole window back.

If that is not enough: recover from history

  1. Open your history with Ctrl+H (Cmd+Y on Mac).
  2. Search for suspended.html.
  3. Each result is one of your suspended tabs. The original address is inside the link, after url=.
  4. Open the entry and copy that address, or simply reopen the page you recognise from its title.

Avoiding this next time

The extension has a Restore all suspended tabs button in its popup. Clicking it before disabling or uninstalling brings every tab back to its real address first, so nothing is lost.
